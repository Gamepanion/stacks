## The service

Gamepanion keeps track of the games you play: the quests you started, your build and the items you found, filed by you or by an AI assistant you connect, and given back without spoilers you didn't ask for. You sign in with your e-mail address.

**Free:** one game, with all its playthroughs.

**Subscription:** every game you play.

**Subscription with Gamepanion's AI:** every game, and the chat in the web app on Gamepanion's own AI, speaking into it included, within a monthly allowance shown in the app. When this month's allowance is used up, the chat on Gamepanion's AI pauses until the first of the next month; your own Anthropic key works meanwhile. Without this plan, the chat in the web app runs on your own Anthropic key, under your agreement with Anthropic.

Reading what you filed, correcting it and deleting it are always free.

## Paying for a subscription

- **Price:** Subscription EUR 4.99 per month or EUR 49.90 per year; Subscription with Gamepanion's AI EUR 9.99 per month or EUR 99.90 per year; VAT included, renewing every month or every year until cancelled. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay.
- **Seller:** payment, invoicing, VAT and the customer portal are handled by Creem (creem.io), which is the seller of record; its terms apply to the purchase. Your payment data is given to Creem, not to Gamepanion.
- **Start:** the subscription applies to your account as soon as Creem confirms the payment, usually within seconds.
- **Cancelling:** at any time, with **Manage subscription** on the subscribe page, which opens Creem's customer portal, or at [creem.io/my-orders/login](https://creem.io/my-orders/login) with the e-mail address you bought with. The subscription runs to the end of the period you paid for.
- **After it ends:** nothing is deleted. One game stays active: the one you choose, and until you choose, the one you played last. Your other games stay readable but take no new sessions until you subscribe again.
